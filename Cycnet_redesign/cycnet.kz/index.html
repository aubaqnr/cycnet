Where Education Meets Industry

CYCNET is a student-led professional community based at SDU University, focused on cybersecurity, computer networks, cloud computing, Linux and system administration, and DevOps.

We work with technology vendors, academic programs, and industry partners to give students access to official training, enterprise technologies, certification pathways, hands-on laboratories, expert mentorship, and real-world projects.

◎

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world IT practice by empowering students through community, mentorship, and hands-on experience.

◉

Our Vision

To be a professional student community where members gain practical skills, leadership experience, and collaborate on real-world IT challenges with global partners.

What We Develop

Core technical and professional directions developed through community learning and hands-on experience.

01

Computer Networks

Enterprise routing and switching, network services, wireless technologies, troubleshooting, and network automation.

02

Cybersecurity

Security operations, network security, ethical hacking, incident response, threat detection, and security engineering.

03

Cloud & DevOps

Cloud infrastructure, containers, CI/CD, automation, infrastructure as code, and modern application platforms.

04

Linux & Systems Administration

Linux administration, enterprise server infrastructure, virtualization, identity services, monitoring, and automation.

05

Professional & Leadership Skills

Project management, technical communication, teamwork, event organization, mentoring, and community leadership.
