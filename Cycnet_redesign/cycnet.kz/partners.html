Partners

Connecting education, technology, and industry through structured learning, practical experience, certifications, and career pathways.

Industry Collaborations

We collaborate with global technology vendors, academic programs, and industry organizations to provide students with professional training, hands-on experience, certification pathways, real-world projects, and career opportunities.

Our partnerships help bridge the gap between university education and the practical skills required by modern IT and cybersecurity teams.

Technology Vendors & Academic Partners

Cisco

Cisco Networking Academy provides structured training in computer networks, infrastructure, cybersecurity, wireless technologies, and network automation. Using official Cisco learning materials, Packet Tracer, laboratory equipment, and practical assignments, students learn to design, configure, secure, and troubleshoot enterprise networks.

More than 1,000 student enrolments have been recorded across the reported academic cohorts.

Fortinet

Fortinet Academy provides official cybersecurity training and practical resources for SDU University students. Participants study network security, firewall technologies, secure access, security operations, and threat protection through structured courses, practical laboratories, and sessions with industry professionals.

More than 80 students have completed training. Selected participants received certification vouchers, and more than 40 Fortinet certifications have been earned.

Check Point

Check Point SecureAcademy introduces students to enterprise network security using Check Point technologies and official learning resources. Students gain practical experience in configuring security policies, firewall rules, VPN connections, access control, logging, monitoring, and threat prevention.

Red Hat

Red Hat Academy supports professional education in Linux administration, automation, containers, and enterprise platforms. Official Red Hat course materials are combined with hands-on practice in the Red Hat Laboratory at SDU University.

TryHackMe

TryHackMe provides interactive cybersecurity laboratories covering networking, Linux, web application security, ethical hacking, digital forensics, incident response, and SOC operations. Through guided learning paths and virtual environments, students practise technical skills and investigate realistic cybersecurity scenarios.

Hack The Box

Hack The Box provides advanced cybersecurity laboratories and realistic environments for offensive and defensive security training. Students improve their skills through practical challenges involving exploitation, privilege escalation, Active Directory security, vulnerability assessment, network defence, and CTF competitions.

Palo Alto Networks

Palo Alto Networks Cybersecurity Academy provides learning resources covering modern network security, cloud security, and security operations technologies. Students explore enterprise firewall administration, security automation, threat detection, and SOC workflows through official learning modules and practical activities.

CompTIA

CompTIA learning pathways help students build vendor-neutral knowledge aligned with internationally recognized IT and cybersecurity certifications. The programs support foundational and intermediate skills in infrastructure, networking, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and IT operations.

Splunk

Splunk Academic Alliance gives students experience with enterprise data analytics, log management, monitoring, and security investigation. Students learn to collect, search, analyze, and visualize machine data while developing practical skills relevant to IT operations and Security Operations Centers.

AWS Academy

AWS Academy brings cloud computing education into the university learning environment through official AWS curricula and practical cloud laboratories. With support from qualified instructors and industry specialists, students develop skills in cloud architecture, deployment, security, operations, and automation.

From Education to Employment

CYCNET prepares students through university education, official vendor training, hands-on laboratories, technical projects, certification preparation, professional mentorship, and industry events.

Qualified and motivated students can be connected with partner companies for internships, industrial practice, capstone projects, junior technical positions, graduate employment, research initiatives, and innovation projects.

Our objective is to help companies discover emerging professionals while giving students a clear pathway from education to industry.

Become a CYCNET Partner

We welcome collaboration with technology vendors, cybersecurity companies, cloud providers, system integrators, financial institutions, telecommunications companies, educational organizations, and other employers interested in developing IT talent.

Together, we can create practical learning opportunities, support professional certification, develop industry-relevant skills, and prepare students for modern technology careers.
